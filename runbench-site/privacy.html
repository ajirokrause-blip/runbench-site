RUNBENCH

Privacy

RUNBENCH is a free beta made by Alexander Krause. Contact: support@runbench.app.

An account is an email address and a password. The password is handled by Supabase Auth. RUNBENCH stores an entitlement record for that account so the beta can tell whether you have accepted access. The account service is Supabase at syfxeeujgygmosgiooiq.supabase.co.

Projects, prompts, generated files, and the API keys you add for image, video, and chat providers stay on your computer. Provider API keys are stored in the operating system credential store. RUNBENCH does not upload those keys to its own server.

When you send a chat or a generation, that request goes from your computer to the provider you connected, using your key. RUNBENCH does not run those provider accounts.

The Mac and Windows installers are files on Cloudflare R2. Downloading them does not send your account to R2.

This beta does not use advertising or a third-party analytics SDK. Stripe checkout exists in the app and is turned off. No payment is collected.

You can stop using RUNBENCH by signing out and deleting the app. To ask for the account email and entitlement record to be deleted, email support@runbench.app.

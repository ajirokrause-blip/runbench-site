RUNBENCH

Privacy

Effective September 29, 2026.

RUNBENCH is a free desktop beta from Alexander Krause. Contact: support@runbench.app.

Account

Creating an account requires an email address and a password. Supabase Auth stores them at syfxeeujgygmosgiooiq.supabase.co. The password is stored as a sign-in credential. We do not keep a separate copy. Sign-in can be refused until that email is confirmed. A sign-in session is stored on your computer, encrypted with the operating system, and refreshed with Supabase while you stay signed in.

Entitlement

When you accept the beta, we store one record for that account: an id, whether access is active, the beta tier, and the time you signed up. Payment fields on that record are empty. While you are signed in, the app contacts Supabase to check that access and to download the current app configuration. That request carries your account token. Supabase can see the account id, the IP address, and the time.

On your computer

Projects, prompts, notes, generated files, and the API keys you add stay on your computer. API keys and the sign-in session are stored with the operating system's encryption. We do not upload projects or API keys to our server. Uninstalling the app does not delete the account. Files on the computer remain until you delete them. The beta is not a backup.

Chat and generation APIs

A chat request goes from your computer to the chat provider you connected, using your key. Those providers are Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google. An image or video generation goes from your computer to the API you connected, using your key. Those APIs are Black Forest Labs (Flux), Ideogram, Runway, and Stability AI. Images you attach to a generation are sent the same way. We do not receive a copy of those requests on our server. Each provider handles the prompt under its own terms.

Midjourney

Midjourney is not called with an API key. The app copies the prompt to your clipboard and opens midjourney.com in the app's browser. What you do on that site, including any login, is between you and Midjourney. That site can set cookies inside the app on your computer. We do not receive your Midjourney login.

ComfyUI

ComfyUI runs on a computer you choose. Traffic to it does not go through our server.

Downloads and updates

The website, the installers, and the app's update check are hosted by Cloudflare. Cloudflare can see the IP address and basic request data for a download or an update check. The update check does not include your email or your projects.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for advertising. We do not use a third-party analytics SDK. We do not track you across other websites, and we do not respond to Do Not Track signals. Stripe checkout in the app is off. No card number is collected. If a paid plan is offered later, this policy will be updated before any charge.

How long

We keep the account and the entitlement record until you ask us to delete them or we close the beta. Supabase and Cloudflare keep their own connection logs to operate and secure their services. Those logs can include IP address, time, and, for Supabase, the account id. We do not use them for advertising. After a deletion, a backup at Supabase may keep a copy for a short time.

Access and deletion

Email support@runbench.app to see what we store, to correct the email on the account, or to delete the account and the entitlement record. We will delete the Supabase login and the entitlement row. We cannot delete files that remain on your computer, or prompts you already sent to a provider.

Age

RUNBENCH is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Outside the United States

We do not aim this beta at the UK or the European Economic Area. If you are there, you can email us to access or delete your account. We have not appointed a representative in those regions.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will change the date at the top and post the new version on this page.
